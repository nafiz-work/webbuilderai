About Our Unwanted Sofa Removal Service

Your old sofa has seen better days, and getting it out of your home can feel like a monumental task. That's where Junk Pro Express comes in. We specialize in quick, hassle-free sofa removal throughout Albuquerque and surrounding communities. Whether you're upgrading your living room, clearing out a rental property, or just ready to say goodbye to that worn-out couch, our team handles everything.

We remove all types of sofas and large seating furniture — from standard 3-seat couches to massive sectionals, sleeper sofas, loveseats, recliners, and futons. It doesn't matter if your sofa is in perfect condition and ready for donation, or if it's broken, stained, or falling apart. We'll take it.

Our service is designed for your convenience. You don't need to drag your sofa outside or even move it to another room. Just point to what needs to go, and our experienced crew will safely maneuver it out of your home — even from tight spaces, upstairs bedrooms, or basements. We protect your walls, floors, and doorways throughout the process.

After we remove your sofa, we don't just dump it. When furniture is still in usable condition, we work with local charities and donation centers to give it a second life. Items that can't be donated are disposed of responsibly at appropriate recycling and disposal facilities. You get a clean space and peace of mind.
