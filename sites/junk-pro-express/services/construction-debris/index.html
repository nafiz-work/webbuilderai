About Our Construction Debris Removal Service

Whether you're a contractor wrapping up a major renovation, a builder clearing a job site, or a homeowner tackling a DIY project, construction debris can pile up fast. Junk Pro Express specializes in fast, efficient construction debris removal throughout Albuquerque and the surrounding areas.

Our experienced crew handles all types of construction waste — from drywall scraps and lumber to concrete, bricks, and roofing materials. We understand that time is money on construction sites, which is why we offer flexible scheduling including same-day pickups to keep your project moving forward.

We serve both residential and commercial clients, from single-room remodels to large-scale commercial construction projects. Our team arrives with the right equipment to safely and efficiently load and haul away your construction debris, no matter the size of the job.

What sets Junk Pro Express apart is our commitment to responsible disposal. We sort recyclable materials like metal, clean wood, and concrete, diverting them from landfills whenever possible. You get a clean job site, and we handle the disposal — the right way.
