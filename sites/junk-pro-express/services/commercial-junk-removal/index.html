About Our Commercial Junk Removal Service

Running a business in Albuquerque means dealing with waste and clutter that can accumulate quickly. Whether you're relocating offices, clearing out a retail space, renovating a warehouse, or simply need regular commercial waste removal, Junk Pro Express is your trusted local partner for all commercial junk removal needs.

Our professional team understands that time is money for your business. That's why we offer flexible scheduling options, including early morning, evening, and weekend appointments to work around your business hours. We arrive on time, work efficiently, and leave your commercial space clean and ready for use.

From single-item pickups to complete building cleanouts, we have the equipment, manpower, and expertise to handle commercial jobs of any size. Our crews are trained to navigate office buildings, retail stores, warehouses, and industrial facilities safely while protecting your property and minimizing disruption to your operations.

We serve all types of businesses throughout Albuquerque and Rio Rancho, including offices, retail stores, restaurants, hotels, property management companies, construction contractors, and more. No commercial junk removal job is too big or too small for our experienced team.

At Junk Pro Express, we're committed to responsible disposal practices. We sort through all items and ensure that recyclable materials are properly recycled, usable items are donated when possible, and the remaining waste is disposed of in compliance with all local regulations.
