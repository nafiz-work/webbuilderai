About Our Furniture Removal Service

When it's time to get rid of old furniture, Junk Pro Express makes it effortless. Whether you're replacing a worn-out sofa, clearing out a bedroom set after a move, or dealing with furniture that's just too heavy to move yourself, we've got you covered. Our experienced team handles all the heavy lifting so you don't have to strain your back or bother friends and family for help.

We work with both residential homeowners and commercial properties throughout Albuquerque and Rio Rancho. From single items like an old recliner or mattress to complete living room sets and office furniture clean-outs, no job is too big or too small. We carefully navigate stairs, tight hallways, and tricky doorways to remove furniture without damaging your property.

What sets Junk Pro Express apart is our commitment to responsible disposal. Furniture in good condition gets donated to local charities where it can help families in need. Items that can't be reused are recycled whenever possible. We believe in keeping usable furniture out of landfills while providing convenient, affordable service to our Albuquerque community.

Need furniture gone fast? We offer same-day and next-day furniture removal when scheduling allows. Just give us a call at 505-301-7315 or fill out our quote form, and we'll provide an upfront price with no hidden fees. Once you approve, we'll handle everything from start to finish.
