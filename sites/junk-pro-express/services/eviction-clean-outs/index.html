About Our Eviction Clean-out Service

Dealing with an eviction is stressful enough without having to worry about clearing out abandoned belongings. At Junk Pro Express, we specialize in complete eviction clean-out services that help Albuquerque landlords and property managers reclaim their properties quickly and efficiently.

Our professional team handles everything left behind—from furniture and appliances to personal belongings and trash. We understand the urgency of getting your rental property back on the market, which is why we offer same-day and next-day service options to minimize your vacancy time and lost income.

Whether you're dealing with a single apartment unit or an entire multi-family property, we have the experience and equipment to handle clean-outs of any size. Our crew is trained to work efficiently while treating your property with care, ensuring we leave the space ready for your next tenant.

We work with landlords, property management companies, and real estate investors throughout Albuquerque and Rio Rancho. Our pricing is transparent, with no hidden fees—we provide a complete quote before any work begins, so you know exactly what to expect.
