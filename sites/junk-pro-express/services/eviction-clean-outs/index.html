About This Service

Professional Eviction Clean-out Services

When tenants leave behind belongings, trash, and debris after an eviction, you need a reliable team to clear the property quickly and thoroughly. Junk Pro Express specializes in eviction clean-outs throughout Albuquerque and surrounding areas, helping landlords and property managers restore their properties to rent-ready condition.

Our experienced crew handles everything from furniture and appliances to personal belongings and accumulated trash. We understand the urgency of getting your property back on the market, which is why we offer same-day service when available and work efficiently to minimize your vacancy time.

Whether you manage a single rental property or an entire portfolio, we provide consistent, professional service with upfront pricing. We'll walk through the property with you, provide a clear quote before any work begins, and leave your space completely cleared and broom-clean.

We serve residential and commercial properties of all sizes across Albuquerque, including apartments, single-family homes, duplexes, and multi-unit buildings. Our team treats every property with respect and discretion, understanding the sensitive nature of eviction situations.

What We Remove & What We Don't

✓ What We Remove

  • ✓ Furniture (sofas, beds, tables, chairs, dressers)
  • ✓ Appliances (refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves)
  • ✓ Electronics (TVs, computers, stereos)
  • ✓ Clothing and personal belongings
  • ✓ Mattresses and box springs
  • ✓ Household trash and debris
  • ✓ Kitchen items and dishes
  • ✓ Toys, books, and miscellaneous items
  • ✓ Outdoor furniture and grills
  • ✓ Boxes and stored items

✕ Exceptions

  • ✕ Hazardous chemicals and solvents
  • ✕ Paints and stains (certain types)
  • ✕ Asbestos-containing materials
  • ✕ Medical waste and sharps
  • ✕ Propane tanks (unless empty)
  • ✕ Items requiring specialized licensing
