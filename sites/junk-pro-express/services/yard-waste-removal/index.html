About Our Yard Waste Removal Service

Maintaining a beautiful outdoor space in Albuquerque's unique high desert climate can generate a lot of yard waste. Whether you're cleaning up after seasonal trimming, tackling a landscaping project, or dealing with storm debris, Junk Pro Express is here to help. Our professional yard waste removal service takes the backache out of yard cleanup.

We handle everything from small pile pickups to full property clearing. Our crew arrives ready to work with all the equipment needed to load and haul away your yard debris quickly and efficiently. You don't need to bag anything, drag it to the curb, or rent a trailer — we do all the heavy lifting while you relax.

Our yard waste removal service is perfect for homeowners, property managers, landscaping companies, and anyone who needs to clear organic debris from their property. Whether you're prepping for a new garden, cleaning up after a tree removal, or simply doing your annual yard maintenance, we've got you covered.

At Junk Pro Express, we're committed to responsible disposal. We take your yard waste to appropriate facilities where it can be composted or recycled into mulch — keeping it out of landfills and giving it a second life. It's good for your property and good for Albuquerque's environment.
