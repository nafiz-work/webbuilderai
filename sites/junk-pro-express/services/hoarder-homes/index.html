About Our Hoarder Home Cleanout Services

At Junk Pro Express, we understand that hoarding situations require a different approach than typical junk removal. Our trained team brings patience, empathy, and complete discretion to every hoarder home cleanout in Albuquerque and the surrounding areas.

Whether you're helping a loved one, managing an inherited property, or taking the brave step of reclaiming your own space, we're here to support you without judgment. We've helped countless families navigate these challenging situations, always respecting the emotional weight that comes with letting go of belongings.

Our approach focuses on collaboration. We work at your pace, help sort items that may be valuable or sentimental, and ensure everything is handled responsibly. Our goal isn't just to clear a space—it's to help restore safety, functionality, and peace of mind to your home.

From single rooms to entire homes, no job is too big or too overwhelming. We bring the manpower, equipment, and experience to transform any space while treating you and your belongings with the dignity you deserve.
