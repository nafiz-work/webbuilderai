About Our Appliance Removal Service

Getting rid of old, bulky appliances can be a real headache. They're heavy, awkward to move, and most regular trash services won't touch them. That's where Junk Pro Express comes in. We specialize in fast, affordable appliance removal throughout Albuquerque and Rio Rancho, handling everything from ancient refrigerators to broken washing machines.

Our trained crew arrives on time, carefully removes your unwanted appliances without damaging your floors or walls, and loads everything onto our truck. You don't lift a finger. Whether you're upgrading your kitchen, dealing with a broken unit, or clearing out a property, we make the process completely hassle-free.

We serve both residential and commercial customers across the Albuquerque metro area. Landlords clearing rental units, property managers updating buildings, or homeowners finally getting rid of that old freezer in the garage — we've got you covered. Same-day service is available for most requests, so you can reclaim your space fast.

What happens to your old appliances after we pick them up? We prioritize responsible disposal. Working units in good condition may be donated to local charities. Everything else goes to certified recycling facilities where metals, plastics, and components are properly processed — keeping them out of landfills and reducing environmental impact.
