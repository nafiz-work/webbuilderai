About Our Appliance Removal Service

When your old appliances break down or you're upgrading to new models, getting rid of the bulky old ones can be a major headache. Refrigerators, washing machines, dryers, and other large appliances are heavy, awkward to move, and can't just be left at the curb. That's where Junk Pro Express comes in.

Our professional appliance removal service is designed for both residential homeowners and commercial property managers throughout Albuquerque and the surrounding areas. Whether you have a single broken dishwasher or a warehouse full of outdated commercial equipment, our experienced crew has the tools and expertise to handle the job safely and efficiently.

We understand that appliances contain materials that require special handling. Refrigerators and air conditioners have refrigerants that must be properly recovered. Many appliances contain metals and components that can be recycled. We work with certified recycling facilities in New Mexico to ensure your old appliances are disposed of in an environmentally responsible manner.

What sets us apart is our commitment to convenience and customer service. We show up when we say we will, provide upfront pricing with no hidden fees, and handle all the heavy lifting ourselves. You don't need to drag that old washer to the garage — we'll pick it up right from where it sits. Our same-day service availability means you don't have to live with that broken appliance blocking your kitchen for weeks.
