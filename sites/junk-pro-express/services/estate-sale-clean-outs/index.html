About This Service

About Our Estate Sale Clean-out Service

Estate sale clean-outs require a unique blend of efficiency, care, and respect. After an estate sale concludes, there's often a significant amount of remaining items that need to be cleared before the property can be sold, rented, or transferred. Junk Pro Express specializes in handling these sensitive situations throughout Albuquerque and the surrounding areas.

Our experienced crew understands that estate clean-outs often come during difficult times. Whether you're settling an estate after a loved one has passed, helping elderly parents downsize, or managing property for an out-of-town family member, we approach every job with compassion and professionalism.

We handle complete property clearing, including furniture, appliances, clothing, household items, garage contents, yard debris, and everything in between. Our team does all the heavy lifting, hauling, and proper disposal—you never have to lift a finger. We work efficiently to clear the entire property in most cases within a single day.

What sets us apart is our commitment to responsible disposal. We coordinate donations for usable items to local Albuquerque charities, properly recycle applicable materials, and only send truly unsalvageable items to the landfill. Upon request, we provide donation receipts for tax purposes, adding value to an already stressful process.

From small condos to large multi-generational homes with basements and garages, no estate clean-out is too big or too complex for Junk Pro Express. We've helped hundreds of Albuquerque families clear properties quickly and respectfully, and we're ready to help yours.
