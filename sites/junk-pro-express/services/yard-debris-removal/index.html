Scope of Service

What We Remove

We handle all types of yard debris so you can reclaim your outdoor space. Here's what our team can and cannot take.

What We Take

  • Tree branches and limbs
  • Leaves and grass clippings
  • Brush and shrub trimmings
  • Dead plants and flowers
  • Storm debris
  • Fallen trees (cut into sections)
  • Weeds and garden waste
  • Mulch and soil (bagged)
  • Small stumps
  • Palm fronds and tumbleweeds

What We Don't Take

  • Large tree stumps (still rooted)
  • Hazardous materials or chemicals
  • Pesticides and herbicides
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Contaminated soil
  • Standing live trees
