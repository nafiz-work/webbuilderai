What We Do

About Our Pre & Post Estate Sale Clean-out Service

Estate sales can be overwhelming, especially when dealing with a lifetime of accumulated belongings. At Junk Pro Express, we specialize in comprehensive estate sale clean-out services that take the burden off your shoulders, whether you're preparing for the sale or clearing out what's left behind.

Our pre-estate sale services help you maximize your sale potential by removing items that won't sell—broken furniture, outdated appliances, garage clutter, and unwanted items that take up valuable display space. We clear the path so your estate sale can shine.

After the sale ends, we return to handle everything that remains. From unsold furniture and household goods to boxes, papers, and general debris, our team efficiently removes and properly disposes of all items. We donate usable items to local Albuquerque charities whenever possible, giving belongings a second life while keeping them out of landfills.

Whether you're an estate sale company, a family handling affairs, or a real estate agent preparing a property for market, Junk Pro Express delivers professional, respectful service with same-day availability throughout Albuquerque and surrounding areas.
