About Our Residential Junk Removal Service

Whether you're decluttering your home, preparing for a move, or simply need to get rid of years of accumulated stuff, Junk Pro Express makes residential junk removal in Albuquerque easy and stress-free. Our professional team handles everything from single-item pickups to whole-home cleanouts.

We understand that your home is your sanctuary, and we treat it with respect. Our crew arrives on time, works efficiently, and leaves your space clean and clutter-free. No job is too big or too small — from clearing out a packed garage to hauling away old furniture that's been collecting dust.

Our residential junk removal service is perfect for homeowners tackling spring cleaning, downsizing for retirement, preparing a home for sale, or simply reclaiming valuable living space. We serve single-family homes, apartments, condos, and townhouses throughout Albuquerque and Rio Rancho.

What sets us apart is our commitment to responsible disposal. We don't just haul everything to the landfill — items in good condition are donated to local charities, and recyclable materials are taken to the appropriate facilities. You get a clean space and the peace of mind that comes with eco-friendly disposal.
