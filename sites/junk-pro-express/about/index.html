Our Story

Who We Are

Junk Pro Express was founded with a simple mission: to help the people of Albuquerque reclaim their spaces from unwanted clutter. We saw how overwhelming junk removal could be for homeowners, property managers, and businesses — and we knew there had to be a better way.

Today, we've grown into one of Albuquerque's most trusted junk removal teams, serving communities throughout the metro area including Rio Rancho, Corrales, and the South Valley. Our team takes pride in every job, treating your property with the same care and respect we'd show our own homes.

What sets us apart? We believe in transparent pricing, showing up on time, and handling everything from start to finish so you don't have to lift a finger. We're not happy until your space is cleared and you're smiling.

As a locally owned and operated business, we're invested in keeping Albuquerque beautiful. We donate usable items to local charities and recycle whenever possible, ensuring your junk finds new life instead of ending up in a landfill.
