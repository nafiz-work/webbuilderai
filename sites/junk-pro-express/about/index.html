Our Story

Who We Are

Junk Pro Express was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves a clean, clutter-free space, and getting rid of junk shouldn't be stressful or complicated. What started as a mission to help our Albuquerque neighbors reclaim their homes has grown into a trusted name throughout the greater metro area.

We understand that behind every pile of junk is a story. Whether you're clearing out a loved one's estate, finally tackling that garage project you've been putting off, or managing a commercial cleanout, we approach every job with respect, care, and efficiency.

Our team lives and works in Albuquerque and Rio Rancho. We know these communities because they're our communities too. That's why we treat every home and business like it belongs to a friend — because in a way, it does.

From day one, we've been committed to doing things the right way: showing up on time, providing upfront pricing, handling your items carefully, and disposing of everything responsibly. It's not just business — it's about building lasting relationships with the people we serve.
