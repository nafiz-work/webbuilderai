About Us

Portland's Locally Owned Junk Removal Team

Solely Junk LLC was founded with a simple mission: provide Portland with reliable, affordable, and eco-conscious junk removal services. We're not a franchise or a big corporation — we're your neighbors.

Our team takes pride in every job, whether it's removing a single couch or clearing out an entire estate. We understand that dealing with junk can be stressful, especially during difficult life transitions. That's why we approach every project with care, respect, and professionalism.
