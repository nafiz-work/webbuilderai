About Us

Your Local Peoria Junk Removal Experts

Canyon State Junk Removal is a locally owned and operated business serving Peoria and the surrounding Arizona communities. We started with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable junk removal services that make our customers' lives easier.

Our team is led by a West Valley firefighter who brings the same dedication, work ethic, and commitment to service that he brings to protecting our community. We treat every job, big or small, with the same level of professionalism and care.

Whether you need a single item removed or a complete property cleanout, we're here to help. We take pride in our excellent communication, fair pricing, and the quality of our work.
