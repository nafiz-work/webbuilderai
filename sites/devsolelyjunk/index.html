What We Haul

Items We Remove

We handle almost anything. Here's a look at what we commonly remove.

At Solely Junk LLC, we make junk removal easy. Whether you're clearing out a single room or tackling a whole property cleanout, we're equipped to handle items of all sizes and types.

Our team comes prepared with the right equipment to safely remove heavy, bulky, or awkward items. You just point to what needs to go, and we take care of the rest.

We're committed to responsible disposal. Items in good condition are donated to local charities, recyclables go to appropriate facilities, and only truly unusable items end up in the landfill.

Items We Cannot Take

Hazardous materials, chemicals, paints, oils, asbestos, and certain regulated items require special disposal and cannot be removed by our service.
