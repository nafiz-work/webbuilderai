Everything you need to know about renting a dumpster in Albuquerque.

How much does dumpster rental cost in Albuquerque?

Our dumpster rental prices are competitive and transparent. We offer various sizes to fit your project needs. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your specific requirements. We pride ourselves on being one of the best values in Albuquerque.

Do you offer same-day dumpster delivery?

Yes! We offer same-day and next-day delivery for most orders in the Albuquerque area. We understand your project timeline is important, and we work hard to accommodate your schedule — even on weekends.

What can I put in a rental dumpster?

You can dispose of construction debris, yard waste, household junk, furniture, appliances (without refrigerants), and general renovation materials. Hazardous materials, tires, batteries, and paint are not accepted. Call us if you're unsure about specific items.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Standard rental periods are typically 3-7 days, but we're flexible! If you need more time, just let us know. We can extend your rental to accommodate your project timeline at reasonable rates.

We proudly serve Albuquerque and the surrounding areas throughout New Mexico. Contact us to confirm service availability in your specific location — we're happy to help!
