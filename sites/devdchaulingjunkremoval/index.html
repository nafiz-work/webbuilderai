What We Haul

Items We Remove

If you can point to it, we can probably haul it. Here's a sample of what we take.

At DC Hauling & Junk Removal, we handle just about everything you need gone. From a single piece of furniture to a complete property cleanout, our team has the equipment and expertise to get the job done quickly and safely.

We believe in responsible disposal. Whenever possible, we donate usable items to local charities and recycle materials to keep them out of landfills. It's our way of giving back to the Machesney Park community while helping the environment.

Not sure if we can take something? Just ask! Send us a photo or give us a call, and we'll let you know right away.

What we can't take: Hazardous materials including paint, chemicals, asbestos, medical waste, and certain electronics. Contact us if you're unsure about any items.
